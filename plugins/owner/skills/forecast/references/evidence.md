# Evidence for owner:forecast

What the sources and this pack's own runs found, with the record of corrections and the numbered source list, moved out of the body of `owner:forecast`. Headings are the sections of the skill each passage came from.

## What is and is not established

- No published agreement or calibration figure for gstack's shadow decider was found; its scoring round lives in a separate evaluation repository [1]. Cite it as a design, not a result.
- The surveyed skill packs do not seal a prediction of the owner's answer before asking or score one afterwards. The nearest analogs are gstack's recommended-versus-chosen question log and owner-set per-question auto-decide preferences [2][3]; its gated "bet" write-back (a dated prediction about the project at a fixed weight, gated off by default, not about the owner's answer and not calibratable) [3]; labeled predicted withdrawals; and ce-pov's rule to form and freeze its own position before consulting peers [7].
- No study was found of an agent forecasting one person's answers to its own questions under a sealed protocol; the closest are permission prediction [21] and words-versus-deeds [22]. The loop is offered as a practice, not an established result. Searches were not exhaustive.
- How well recommendations predicted one owner, read as implicit, unsealed forecasts (no probabilities recorded; readers' tallies, unverified as scores; self-use): about 3 of 6 recommended defaults held in one sitting; on a numbered shortlist the agent recommended items the owner declined and did not recommend the one this skill is built on; on five borderline leans, two missed; the agent recommended against building this skill and was overruled; several late process recommendations were overruled or redirected. Hits clustered on curated, reversible bundles, clear draft verdicts and "shall I go ahead" closers; the owner changed three or four of a curator's recommended calls on one build. Three sealed predictions about a component all hit, which says little. The lesson: recommending is not forecasting, and unrecorded hits teach nothing.
- Provisional, pending the anneal with `decide:docket` (made under an explicit per-job instruction to accept the curator's recommendations; not the owner's rulings): the opt-in blind sample (described, not switched on); the silence-default rule (four conditions); and this skill's home among its siblings (it owns the ladder, the forecast loop, the ask format and the consent rules; it points to the queue, bar and prereg skills). A proposed shared home for team reports would also hold the asking half (the go-ahead, consent and which decisions are the owner's), which overlaps this skill's ladder, owner-only list and consent rules; where asking lives is a matter for the integration pass.
