# The raised eyebrow

Ask who has tried this and what happened to them, from two sources, each blind to the other and to how keen anyone is.

**The weights.** One fresh agent on the strongest model, given the plan or the requirements in neutral words. Ask it: "Do not search; answer from what you know, and say where you are unsure. What would the people who know this area best say on hearing this? What do they actually do instead? What is the likeliest reason it is unnecessary, and the likeliest reason it fails in practice? What would you cut, and what is missing? Tried-and-true practice only: no invention, no encouragement. Be blunt and specific." Add the questions this plan raises, numbered, and a word limit.

**The internet.** Search for the case against ("why not", "problems with", "we stopped doing") as well as for whether it already exists. For costly work make it a research fan-out, and have a fresh agent check that each cited page says what it is cited for. Search in generic terms: nothing private goes into a query.

Show the user both as they came, then ask what they still want. What they answer is a requirement like any other; a method the user named is used as named. With no way to spawn an agent, say so: your own opinion is not the eyebrow.
